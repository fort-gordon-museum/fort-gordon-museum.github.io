P	SPECIAL MESSAGE une 6, 1944. D-Day at Normandy. It was the Allied invasion of northern France. Most Americans are familiar with the event and many have seen the movie The Longest Day. But how many Signal soldiers know the Signal connection with the producer of this epic war film, Darryl F. Zanuck? Darryl Zanuck was born in Nebraska in 1902. He moved to California at age six and began working in films by eight. At fifteen he lied about his age to join the Nebraska National Guard and ended up serving on the Mexican Border and in France during WWI. He was only eighteen when the war ended. After the war Zanuck did some writing and sold his first movie script in 1922 to a Hollywood producer. He soon began working with Warner Brothers as a script writer.
