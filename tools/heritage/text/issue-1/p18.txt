P	All of its combat scenes were real. Zanuck received a Legion of Merit for his role. He continued on to supervise production of films during the fighting in Italy, returning to Twentieth Century Fox studio in 1944.
P	In 1962 he completed what many consider his greatest film accomplishment, The Longest Day. Filmed on location in Corsica and Normandy as well as on sets in Paris, the film used the services of fortyeight technical supervisors and a who’s who of Hollywood legends such as John Wayne, Robert Mitchum, Henry Fonda, Eddie Albert, Richard Burton, Sean Connery, Robert Ryan, Sal Mineo, Paul Anka, Red Buttons, Roddy McDowell, Fabian, Edmund O’Brian, and Robert Wagner. At a cost of $10 million, it was a major production and a box office success with $40 million.
P	Zanuck continued producing films such as The Sound of Music (1965), Planet of the Apes (1968), and Tora! Tora! Tora! (1970). He passed away in Palm Springs, California, in 1971.
