P	Based at Patch Barracks, Stuttgart (Vaihingen), Germany, the SSD's mission was to "provide initial wartime communications support to Commander, SOCEUR (COMSOCEUR) and US Special Operations liaison elements at various NATO HQ." Additionally, SSD was tasked to "provide C2 communications support to COMSOCEUR in support of contingency and special operations. A third task was to serve as the personal communications support for the Deputy Commander, US European Command. (3)
P	Almost immediately, SSD was tested in real-world missions. Their first operational mission supported the 1986 Operation ELDORADO CANYON (the US response to Libya based terrorist attacks including a Berlin nightclub bombing). Other early missions included support to hostage rescue operations (1986 to 1991), Operation STEEL BOX (removal of chemical weapons from Europe), and deployments to Turkey for Operations DESERT STORM, and PROVIDE COMFORT, both in 1991. In this latter mission, SSD earned the Joint Meritorious Unit Award. (4)
P	This unique support was only possible because of the close coordination between SSD and the SOCEUR J6 Directorate. While SSD provided the manpower and operators, the SOCEUR J6 spearheaded the innovation and secured the resources needed. Understanding the requirements of the SOCEUR deployable HQs is where SOCEUR J6 developed the ideas and systems that SSD would train to proficiently operate. These requirements served to fuel creative application of technology, training methods, and deployment techniques key to both their past successes and opportunities for the future.
