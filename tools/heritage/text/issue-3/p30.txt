P	The passing of the 34th recipient of the Bronze Order of Mercury. LTC (Ret.) Tom Hoyle was laid to rest Yesterday at the Massachusetts National cemetery at Joint Base Cape Cod. Of note, as we as a regiment prepare for the next iteration of Technet Augusta, then MAJ Tom Hoyle was the original project officer for the first
P	Signal Symposium way back when it first started. As the first company commander for the newly relocated Signal Student Officer Company when it first stood up at Fort Gordon, he set the standard for generations of signal personnel. A Vietnam veteran of two tours, he finished his last duty assignment in Signal Towers at the TCM Multifunctional Computers setting the stage for what is now USCYBERCOM. Thanks LTC (Ret.) Thomas L Hoyle for your untiring duty to God, Country, and Family. Dad…As we say in the US Army Signal Corps: “Meet you on the high Ground!!!” RIP….as the third Generation of The Hoyle Clan are carrying on the military Tradition in both the Navy and Army.

P	It is with deep regret that we inform you of the death of Brigadier General William J. Kennedy, USA, Retired, who passed away on 16 October 2020 at the age of 94. Brigadier General Kennedy retired in 1979 after over 28 years of service. During the latter part of his career he served as Assistant Commandant, United States Army Signal School, Fort Gordon, Georgia and later as Deputy Controller, Central Operating Authority, Supreme Headquarters Allied Powers, Europe.
