1st Signal Center

Lineage and honors as of 3 May 2006

Lineage

Constituted 1 July 1968 in the Regular Army as the 1st Signal Center

Activated 22 November 1968 in Vietnam

Inactivated 24 January 1969 in Vietnam

Activated 16 October 2005 at Fort Belvoir, Virginia
