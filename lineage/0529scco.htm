Lineage and Honors

529th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 25 April 2007

Lineage

Constituted 24 May 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 529th Signal Operation Company

Activated 15 June 1944 at Camp Crowder, Missouri

Inactivated 31 May 1946 in Korea

Activated 27 January 1950 on Guam as the 529th Signal Operations Company

Inactivated 14 March 1950 on Guam

Redesignated 20 September 1950 as the 529th Signal Operation Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 2 October 1950 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Reorganized and redesignated 13 March 1953 as the 529th Signal Company

Inactivated 20 December 1957 at Fort Carson, Colorado

Activated 10 June 1958 at Fort Sill, Oklahoma

Inactivated 16 May 1966 at Fort Hood, Texas

Activated 16 January 2007 at Fort Sill, Oklahoma
