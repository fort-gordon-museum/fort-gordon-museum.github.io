Lineage and Honors

94th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 20 November 2008

Lineage

Constituted 17 April 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 94th Signal Radar Maintenance Unit, Type A

Activated 15 May 1944 in New Guinea

Inactivated 25 October 1945 in the Philippine Islands

Redesignated 29 September 1954 as the 94th Signal Detachment and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 15 November 1954 at Fort Bliss, Texas

Inactivated 25 March 1963 at Fort Bliss, Texas

Activated 24 June 1963 at Fort Bliss, Texas

Inactivated 24 June 1964 at Fort Bliss, Texas

Activated 8 August 1964 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Inactivated 25 November 1968 in Vietnam

Activated 5 August 1969 in Thailand

Inactivated 30 June 1971 in Thailand

Activated 16 October 1991 in Panama

Redesignated 28 April 1997 as the 94th Signal Company

Inactivated 17 October 2003 at Fort Buchanan, Puerto Rico

Activated 16 October 2008 at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri
