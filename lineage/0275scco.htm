Lineage and Honors

275th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 23 September 2010

Lineage

Constituted 13 January 1941 in the Regular Army as the 76th Signal Construction Company

Activated 1 May 1941 at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri

Redesignated 7 August 1941 as the 275th Signal Construction Company

Reorganized and redesignated 1 June 1945 as the 275th Signal Light Construction Company

Inactivated 14 June 1946 in Germany

Redesignated 4 April 1960 as the 275th Signal Company

Activated 1 May 1960 in France

Inactivated 15 May 1969 in Belgium

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 30 June 1971 in Vietnam

Activated 1 July 1974 in Korea

Campaign Participation Credit

World War II

  • Normandy
  • Northern France
  • Rhineland
  • Central Europe

Vietnam

  • Winter-Spring 1970
  • Sanctuary Counteroffensive
  • Counteroffensive, Phase VII

Decorations and Unit Citations
