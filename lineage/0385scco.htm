Lineage

Constituted 18 March 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 385th Signal Company, Air Force

Activated 15 April 1944 in England

Redesignated 9 September 1944 as the 385th Signal Service Company

Inactivated 3 October 1945 at Camp Patrick Henry, Virginia

Redesignated 1 March 1963 as the 385th Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 27 March 1963 at Fort Lee, Virginia

Inactivated 18 August 1965 at Fort Lee, Virginia

Activated 25 February 1966 at Fort Campbell, Kentucky

Inactivated 15 July 1985 at Fort Gordon, Georgia

Activated 16 April 1989 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona
