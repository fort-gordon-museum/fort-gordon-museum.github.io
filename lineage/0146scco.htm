Lineage and Honors

146th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 1 June 2007

Lineage

Constituted 8 January 1942 in the Army of the United States as the 146th Signal Armored Company

Redesignated 24 January 1942 as the 146th Armored Signal Company

Activated 15 February 1942 at Fort Knox, Kentucky, as an element of the 6th Armored Division

Inactivated 19 September 1945 at Camp Shanks, New York

Allotted 5 September 1950 to the Regular Army and activated at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri

Inactivated 16 March 1956 at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri

Relieved 15 March 1960 from assignment to the 6th Armored Division

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam as the 146th Signal Company

Inactivated 19 March 1973 in Vietnam

Activated 16 March 2007 in Germany
