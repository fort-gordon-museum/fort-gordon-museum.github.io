Lineage and Honors

21st Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 20 November 2008

Lineage

Constituted 30 January 1946 in the Army of the United States as the 21st Signal Operation Company

Activated 25 February 1946 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Inactivated 15 October 1946 at Fort Jay, New York

Redesignated 16 April 1951 as the 21st Signal Operations Platoon, allotted to the Regular Army, and activated in Austria

Redesignated 20 June 1952 as the 21st Signal Service Company

Redesignated 16 February 1953 as the 21st Signal Company

Inactivated 13 November 1967 in Italy

Activated 16 February 1973 at Fort Richardson, Alaska

Inactivated 15 April 1986 at Fort Richardson, Alaska

Activated 16 June 1994 at Fort Richardson, Alaska

Inactivated 16 December 2006 at Fort Wainwright, Alaska

Activated 16 April 2007 at Fort Lewis, Washington
