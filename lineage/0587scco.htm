Lineage and Honors

587th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 24 June 2009

Lineage

Constituted 22 February 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 587th Signal Depot Company

Activated 30 March 1944 at Camp Pickett, Virginia

Inactivated 20 January 1946 in the Philippine Islands

Redesignated 23 March 1966 as the 587th Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 1 June 1966 at Fort Irwin, California

Inactivated 15 December 1970 in Vietnam

Activated 1 July 1974 in Germany
