Lineage and Honors

656th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 26 April 2011

Lineage

Constituted 1 October 2004 in the Pennsylvania Army National Guard as the 656th Signal Company

Organized and Federally recognized 5 July 2006 at Torrance as an element of the 56th Brigade Combat Team, 28th Infantry Division

Ordered into active Federal service 19 September 2008 at Torrance; released from active Federal service 23 October 2009 and reverted to state control

HOME STATION: Torrance
