Lineage

Constituted 10 August 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 3257th Signal Service Company

Activated 25 August 1944 in England

Inactivated 25 November 1945 in Germany

Redesignated 25 March 1953 as the 257th Signal Company, allotted to the Regular Army, and activated at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Inactivated 25 June 1959 at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Activated 12 August 1963 in France

Inactivated 12 November 1967 in Germany

Activated 1 July 1974 in Korea

Inactivated 16 October 1995 in Korea

Redesignated 27 January 2005 as the 6th Signal Center

Activated 16 October 2006 in Korea
