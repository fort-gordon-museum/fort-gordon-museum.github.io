Lineage

Constituted 13 January 1941 in the Regular Army as the 21st Signal Operation Company

Activated 10 February 1941 at Fort George G. Meade, Maryland

Redesignated 7 August 1941 as the 226th Signal Operation Company

Reorganized and redesignated 1 December 1945 as the 226th Signal Service Company

Redesignated 20 March 1953 as the 226th Signal Company

Inactivated 28 March 1955 in Korea

Activated 15 October 1957 in Korea

Inactivated 28 February 1978 in Korea

Activated 16 October 1997 in Korea

Inactivated 15 October 2005 in Korea

Activated 16 October 2011 at Fort Drum, New York
