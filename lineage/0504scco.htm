Lineage and Honors

504th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 14 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 24 October 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 3350th Signal Base Maintenance Company and activated in France

Inactivated 25 October 1945 in the Philippines

Redesignated 5 July 1949 as the 504th Signal Base Maintenance Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 7 September 1949 at the Sacramento Signal Depot, California

Reorganized and redesignated 4 October 1954 as the 504th Signal Company

Inactivated 25 June 1966 at the Sacramento Army Depot, California

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 6 September 1971 in Vietnam

Activated 12 December 1992 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1994 in Germany

Activated 16 October 2011 in Germany
