Lineage

Constituted 11 May 1942 in the Army of the United States as the 270th Signal Construction Company

Activated 1 September 1943 at Camp Crowder, Missouri

Reorganized and redesignated 15 February 1944 as the 270th Signal Heavy Construction Company

Inactivated 24 October 1945 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Redesignated 17 April 1961 as the 270th Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 25 April 1961 at Fort Ord, California

Inactivated 24 September 1963 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Activated 20 May 1968 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Inactivated 1 June 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 1 May 1975 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Germany

Activated 17 June 2006 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina
