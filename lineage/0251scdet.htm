Lineage and Honors

251st Signal Detachment

Lineage and honors as of 30 September 2009

Lineage

Constituted 18 February 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 251st Signal Radar Maintenance Unit, Type C

Activated 10 July 1944 at Camp Davis, North Carolina

Inactivated 15 January 1946 on Saipan

Redesignated 23 March 1966 as the 251st Signal Detachment and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 1 June 1966 at Fort McClellan, Alabama

Inactivated 14 October 1970 in Vietnam

Activated 16 October 2002 in Korea
