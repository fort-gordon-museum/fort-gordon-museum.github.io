Lineage and Honors

228th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 20 November 2008

Lineage

Constituted 2 September 1941 in the Regular Army as the 228th Signal Operation Company

Activated 17 September 1941 in the Philippine Islands

Surrendered in 1942 to Japanese Forces in the Philippine Islands

Inactivated 2 April 1946 in the Philippine Islands

Redesignated 9 November 1954 as the 228th Signal Company

Activated 6 January 1955 at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Inactivated 16 November 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 1 July 1974 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Germany

Activated 16 October 2003 in Kuwait
