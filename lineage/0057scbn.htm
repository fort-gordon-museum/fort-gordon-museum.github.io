Lineage and Honors

57th SIGNAL BATTALION

Lineage and honors as of 22 February 2011

Lineage

Constituted 18 October 1927 in the Regular Army as the 57th Signal Battalion

Activated 10 February 1941 at Camp Edwards, Massachusetts

Inactivated 29 September 1945 in Germany

Activated 16 May 1966 at Fort Hood, Texas
