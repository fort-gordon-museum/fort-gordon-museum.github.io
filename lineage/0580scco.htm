Lineage and Honors

580th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 28 March 2008

Lineage

Constituted 17 December 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 580th Signal Depot Company

Activated 20 January 1944 at Fort Ord, California

Inactivated 10 June 1946 in Austria

Redesignated 21 July 1965 as the 580th Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 1 August 1965 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Inactivated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Activated 1 March 1991 in Saudi Arabia
