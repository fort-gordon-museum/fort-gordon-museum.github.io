Lineage and Honors

525th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 1 February 2011

Lineage

Constituted 1 October 1933 in the Regular Army as the 20th Radio Intelligence Company

Activated 1 June 1941 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Redesignated 10 June 1941 as the 125th Signal Radio Intelligence Company

Reorganized and redesignated 1 September 1945 as the 125th Signal Service Company

Inactivated 23 December 1945 on Okinawa

Redesignated 9 May 1949 as the 525th Signal Service Company

Activated 1 June 1949 in the Philippine Islands

Inactivated 10 March 1950 in the Philippine Islands

Redesignated 7 June 1954 as the 525th Signal Company

Activated 15 July 1954 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Inactivated 9 December 1957 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 10 June 1958 at Fort Bliss, Texas

Inactivated 20 July 1966 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 30 June 1971 in Vietnam

Inactivated 20 August 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 16 October 2010 at Miami, Florida
