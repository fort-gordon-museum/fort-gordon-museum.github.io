Lineage and Honors

5th SIGNAL CENTER

Lineage and honors as of 31 January 2012

Lineage

Constituted 13 January 1941 in the Regular Army as the 4th Signal Construction Company

Activated 1 June 1941 at Fort Ord, California

Redesignated 7 August 1941 as the 255th Signal Construction Company

Reorganized and redesignated 12 June 1945 as the 255th Signal Light Construction Company

Inactivated 30 June 1946 in Germany

Redesignated 13 December 1948 as the 593d Signal Operation Company

Activated 15 January 1949 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Inactivated 26 October 1950 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Redesignated 26 November 1958 as the 593d Signal Company

Activated 15 January 1959 at Fort Riley, Kansas

Inactivated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Activated 1 July 1974 in Germany

Inactivated 30 September 1984 in Germany

Activated 16 October 1990 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Inactivated 15 January 1998 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Redesignated 27 January 2005 as the 5th Signal Center

Activated 16 October 2006 in Germany
