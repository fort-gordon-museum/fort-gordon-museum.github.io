Lineage and Honors

414th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 14 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 29 August 1940 in the Regular Army as the 314th Signal Aviation Company

Activated 20 September 1940 at McChord Field, Washington

Redesignated 26 March 1941 as the 314th Signal Company, Air Wing

Redesignated 17 October 1941 as the 414th Signal Company, Aviation

Inactivated 31 December 1945 in Germany

Redesignated 2 March 1967 as the 414th Signal Company

Activated 10 June 1967 at Fort George G. Meade, Maryland

Inactivated 30 April 1983 at Fort George G. Meade, Maryland

Activated 1 October 1993 in Germany

Inactivated 30 September 2001 in Germany

Activated 16 October 2011 at Fort Stewart, Georgia

Campaign Participation Credit

World War II

  • Northern France

Decorations and Unit Citations

Meritorious Unit Commendation (Army), Streamer embroidered EUROPEAN THEATER

Army Superior Unit Award, Streamer embroidered 1999-2000

Cited in the Order of the Day of the Belgian Army for action on the Normandy Beaches
