404th Signal Company

404th Signal Company

Constituted 13 August 1944 in the Army of the United States as Company B, 90th Signal Operation Battalion

Activated 25 August 1944 at Camp Shelby, Mississippi

Redesignated 29 May 1945 as the 4034th Signal Operation Company

Inactivated 21 November 1945 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Redesignated 10 November 1948 as the 404th Signal Telephone and Telegraph Company and allotted to the Organized Reserve Corps

Activated 24 February 1949 at Waterloo, Iowa

Reorganized and redesignated 3 October 1950 as the 404th Signal Operations Company

Reorganized and redesignated 15 April 1952 as the 404th Signal Support Company

(Organized Reserve Corps redesignated 9 July 1952 as the Army Reserve)

Reorganized and redesignated 1 February 1954 as the 404th Signal Company

Ordered into active military service 15 October 1961 at Waterloo, Iowa; released from active military service 14 August 1962 and reverted to reserve status

Inactivated 15 September 1994 at Waterloo, Iowa

Activated 16 September 2012 at Fort Dix, New Jersey
