Lineage and Honors

286th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 25 January 2008

Lineage

Constituted 11 July 1942 in the Army of the United States as the 286th Signal Company, Amphibian

Activated 15 July 1942 at Camp Edwards, Massachusetts

Redesignated 10 May 1943 as the 286th Signal Company

Reorganized and redesignated 5 April 1944 as the 286th Joint Assault Signal Company

Inactivated 14 May 1948 at Fort Emory, California

Redesignated 16 October 1993 as the 286th Signal Company, allotted to the Regular Army, and activated at Fort Bliss, Texas
