Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 335th Signal Command

Lineage and honors as of 16 August 2007

Lineage

Headquarters and Headquarters Company

Constituted 13 February 1953 in the Army Reserve as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 335th Signal Group

Activated 28 February 1953 at Atlanta, Georgia

Reorganized and redesignated 31 December 1953 as Headquarters and Headquarters Detachment, 335th Signal Group

Reorganized and redesignated 30 June 1954 as Headquarters, 335th Signal Group

Reorganized and redesignated 31 March 1955 as Headquarters and Headquarters Detachment, 335th Signal Group

Ordered into active military service 15 October 1961 at Atlanta, Georgia; released from active military service 3 August 1962 and reverted to reserve status

Location changed 19 April 1971 to East Point, Georgia

Reorganized and redesignated 15 March 1972 as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 335th Signal Group

Redesignated 16 October 1984 as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 335th Signal Brigade

Reorganized and redesignated 16 April 1986 as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 335th Signal Command

Elements ordered into active military service 2001-2006 for the War on Terrorism
