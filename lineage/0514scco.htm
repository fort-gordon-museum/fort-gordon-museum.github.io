Lineage and Honors

514th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 4 November 2011

Lineage

Constituted 23 November 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 3373d Signal Service Company

Activated 28 November 1944 at Camp Crowder, Missouri

Inactivated 31 May 1947 in Korea

Redesignated 27 June 1947 as the 514th Signal Service Company

Activated 20 July 1947 in Korea

Inactivated 25 January 1949 in Korea

Activated 25 January 1950 in Japan

Inactivated 15 March 1950 in Japan

Activated 2 October 1950 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey, and allotted to the Regular Army

Reorganized and redesignated 9 March 1953 as the 514th Signal Company

Inactivated 1 May 1957 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 22 March 1968 at Fort Riley, Kansas

Inactivated 18 January 1971 at Fort Riley, Kansas

Activated 30 September 1978 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Inactivated 15 June 2006 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Activated 16 October 2010 at Camp Roberts, California
