Lineage

Constituted 19 March 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 3131st Signal Service Company and activated in Algeria

Reorganized and redesignated 24 February 1945 as the 3131st Signal Photographic Platoon

Inactivated 6 January 1946 in Germany

Redesignated 8 September 1954 as the 293d Signal Company, allotted to the Regular Army, and activated at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Inactivated 1 May 1957 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 12 August 1963 in France

Inactivated 13 November 1967 in Germany

Activated 1 July 1974 in Korea
