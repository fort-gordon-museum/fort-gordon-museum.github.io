Lineage and Honors

334th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 14 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 6 January 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 334th Signal Company, Wing

Activated 10 January 1943 at Pinedale, California

Inactivated 7 November 1945 at Camp Pinedale, California

Allotted 6 November 1967 to the Regular Army; concurrently redesignated as the 334th Signal Company and activated in Thailand

Inactivated 1 July 1969 in Thailand

Activated 1 February 1980 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Inactivated 15 July 1987 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Assigned 16 September 2000 to the 2d Infantry Division and activated at Fort Lewis, Washington

Relieved 16 October 2006 from assignment to the 2d Infantry Division and assigned to the 3d Brigade Combat Team, 2d Infantry Division
