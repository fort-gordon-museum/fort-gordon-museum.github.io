Lineage and Honors

73d SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 14 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 31 May 1945 in the Army of the United States as the 3164th Signal Service Company

Activated 10 July 1945 on Guam

Redesignated 30 June 1947 as the 73d Signal Service Company

Inactivated 12 September 1947 on Guam

Redesignated 9 June 1953 as the 73d Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 1 July 1953 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Inactivated 6 May 1965 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 16 October 2011 at Schofield Barracks, Hawaii

Campaign Participation Credit

World War II

  • Asiatic-Pacific Theater,
  • Streamer without inscription

Decorations and Unit Citations
