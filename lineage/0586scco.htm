Lineage and Honors

586th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 14 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 12 January 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 586th Signal Depot Company

Activated 29 January 1944 at Fort Jackson, South Carolina

Inactivated 28 February 1946 in Japan

Redesignated 28 November 1951 as the 586th Signal Service Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 20 December 1951 at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Reorganized and redesignated 11 August 1952 as the 586th Signal Support Company

Reorganized and redesignated 1 November 1954 as the 586th Signal Company

Inactivated 15 December 1970 in Vietnam

Activated 31 May 1972 in Vietnam

Inactivated 29 June 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 16 September 2006 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina
