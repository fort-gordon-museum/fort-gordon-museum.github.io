Lineage and Honors

501st SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 23 September 2010

Lineage

Constituted 15 March 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 3163d Signal Service Company

Activated 20 March 1944 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Redesignated 1 April 1947 as the 501st Signal Service Company

Reorganized and redesignated 22 August 1951 as the 501st Signal Radio Relay Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Reorganized and redesignated 2 February 1952 as the 501st Signal Service Company

Reorganized and redesignated 9 March 1953 as the 501st Signal Company

Inactivated 23 March 1958 at Fort Devens, Massachusetts

Activated 1 July 1974 in Korea

Campaign Participation Credit

World War II

  • Central Europe
