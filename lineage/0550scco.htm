Lineage and Honors

550th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 12 May2009

Lineage

Constituted 30 August 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 550th Signal Base Depot Company

Activated 15 September 1944 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Inactivated 14 March 1946 on Saipan

Activated 26 April 1947 on Guam

Inactivated 12 September 1947 on Guam

Redesignated 7 March 1951 as the 550th Signal Radio Relay Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 1 July 1951 in Germany

Reorganized and redesignated 5 January 1953 as the 550th Signal Company

Inactivated 20 October 1953 in Germany

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 19 March 1973 in Vietnam

Activated 1 March 1991 in Saudi Arabia
