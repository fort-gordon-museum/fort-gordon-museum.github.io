Lineage and Honors

589th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 1 February 2011

Lineage

Constituted 1 August 1940 in the Regular Army as the 1st Signal Company (Repair) and activated at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Redesignated 1 October 1940 as the 1st Signal Repair Company

Redesignated 7 August 1941 as the 175th Signal Repair Company

Inactivated 19 November 1945 at Camp Patrick Henry, Virginia

Redesignated 29 August 1949 as the 589th Signal Construction Company

Activated 18 October 1949 in Germany

Inactivated 20 October 1953 in Germany

Redesignated 5 April 1955 as the 589th Signal Company

Activated 30 April 1955 at Fort Benning, Georgia

Inactivated 20 December 1966 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 26 January 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 1 July 1974 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Germany

Activated 16 March 2007 in Germany
