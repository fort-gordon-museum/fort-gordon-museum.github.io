Lineage and Honors

298th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 23 December 2010

Lineage

Constituted 8 December 1942 in the Army of the United States as the 298th Signal Installation Company

Activated 15 June 1943 at Camp Crowder, Missouri

Reorganized and redesignated 26 May 1945 as the 298th Signal Service Company

Inactivated 28 December 1945 in Germany

Redesignated 16 February 1953 as the 298th Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 25 March 1953 at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Inactivated 3 December 1954 at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Activated 10 March 1955 in France

Inactivated 10 June 1965 in France

Activated 15 August 1958 in Vietnam

Inactivated 30 July 1971 in Vietnam

Activated 4 May 1973 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Germany

Activated 16 October 2010 at Fort Detrick, Maryland
