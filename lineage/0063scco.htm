Lineage and Honors

63d SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 18 March 2011

Lineage

Constituted 12 April 1946 in the Army of the United States as the 126th Signal Service Battalion

Activated 31 May 1946 in Germany

Reorganized and redesignated 20 June 1947 as the 63d Signal Service Company

Inactivated 20 October 1947 in Germany

Activated 1 October 1948 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Inactivated 25 January 1951 in Alaska

Redesignated 9 June 1953 as the 63d Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 1 July 1953 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Inactivated 25 June 1959 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 16 January 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 16 August 2008 at Fort Lewis, Washington
