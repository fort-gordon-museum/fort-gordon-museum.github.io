Lineage and Honors

252d Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 26 September 2002

Lineage

Constituted 2 September 1941 in the Regular Army as the 252d Signal Construction Company (Philippine Scouts)

Activated 17 September 1941 in the Philippine Islands

Surrendered 9 April 1942 to Japanese forces

Inactivated 2 April 1946 in the Philippine Islands

Redesignated 3 August 1962 as the 252d Signal Company

Activated 8 August 1962 in the Canal Zone

Inactivated 2 June 1972 in the Canal Zone

Activated 31 January 1974 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Germany

Activated 18 October 1997 in Panama

Campaign Participation Credit

World War II-AP

  • Philippine Islands
