Lineage

Constituted 13 January 1941 in the Regular Army as the 2d Signal Repair Company

Activated 10 February 1941 at Fort Ord, California

Redesignated 7 August 1941 as the 176th Signal Repair Company

Inactivated 30 May 1947 on Okinawa

Activated 1 April 1951 in Germany

Reorganized and redesignated 15 June 1954 as the 176th Signal Company

Inactivated 1 October 1961 in Germany

Activated 25 March 1963 at Fort Chaffee, Arkansas

Inactivated 15 June 1991 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Activated 16 May 1993 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Inactivated 15 June 1999 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Assigned 16 January 2002 to the 25th Infantry Division and activated at Fort Lewis, Washington

Relieved 31 August 2006 from the 25th Infantry Division and assigned to the 1st Brigade Combat Team, 25th Infantry Division
