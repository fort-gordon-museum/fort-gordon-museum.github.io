Lineage and Honors

333d SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 15 November 2006

Lineage

Constituted 6 January 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 333d Signal Company, Wing

Activated 10 January 1943 at Pinedale, California

Reorganized and redesignated 1 August 1943 as the 333d Signal Company, Troop Carrier Wing

Inactivated 15 May 1946 in Japan

Redesignated 27 January 1967 as the 333d Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 25 February 1967 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Inactivated 30 April 1983 at Fort Gordon, Georgia

Activated 16 October 1992 in Japan
