Lineage and Honors

HEADQUARTERS AND HEADQUARTERS COMPANY, 505th SIGNAL BRIGADE

Lineage and honors as of 17 October 2011

Lineage

Constituted 28 July 1944 in the Army of the United States as Headquarters and Headquarters Detachment, 3146th Signal Service Group

Activated 29 July 1944 in France

Inactivated 10 July 1946 in Germany

Redesignated 30 December 1946 as Headquarters and Headquarters Detachment, 505th Signal Service Group, and activated at Fort Shafter, Hawaii

Inactivated 18 October 1948 at Fort Shafter, Hawaii

Redesignated 4 February 1952 as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 505th Signal Group, and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 5 March 1952 at Camp San Luis Obispo, California

Reorganized and redesignated 23 November 1953 as Headquarters and Headquarters Detachment, 505th Signal Group

Inactivated 10 September 1965 in Germany

Withdrawn 9 February 2009 from the Regular Army and allotted to the Army Reserve; concurrently redesignated as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 505th Signal Brigade

Activated 16 September 2011 at Las Vegas, Nevada
