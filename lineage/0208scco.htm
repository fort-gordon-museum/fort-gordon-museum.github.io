Lineage and Honors

208th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 14 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 19 February 1942 in the Regular Army as the 208th Signal Depot Company

Activated 18 May 1942 at Camp Crowder, Missouri

Inactivated 28 November 1945 at Camp Kilmer, New Jersey

Activated 1 August 1946 in Alaska

Reorganized and redesignated 15 March 1947 as the 208th Signal Service Company

Inactivated 15 October 1949 in Alaska

Redesignated 7 June 1954 as the 208th Signal Company

Activated 3 August 1954 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Inactivated 1 May 1957 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 24 September 1960 in Germany

Inactivated 24 March 1964 in Germany

Activated 24 June 1964 at Fort Lee, Virginia

Inactivated 1 September 1965 at Fort George G. Meade, Maryland

Activated 24 February 1966 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Inactivated 20 September 1968 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Activated 16 December 1992 at Fort Polk, Louisiana

Inactivated 15 September 2006 at Fort Bliss, Texas

Activated 16 October 2011 at Fort Bliss, Texas
