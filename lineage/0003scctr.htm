Lineage and Honors

3d Signal Center

Lineage and honors as of 15 April 2009

Lineage

Constituted 18 December 1944 in the Army of the United States as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 3911th Signal Base Depot

Activated 25 January 1945 in New Guinea

Inactivated 31 May 1946 in the Philippines

Redesignated 26 April 1948 as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 803d Signal Base Depot, and allotted to the Organized Reserve Corps

Activated 20 May 1948 at New York, New York

(Organized Reserve Corps redesignated 9 July 1952 as the Army Reserve)

Reorganized and redesignated 1 February 1955 as Headquarters and Headquarters Company, 803d Signal Depot

Inactivated 31 January 1968 at New York, New York

Redesignated 15 June 2004 as the 3d Signal Depot; concurrently withdrawn from the Army Reserve and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 16 October 2005 in Kuwait

Campaign Participation Credit

World War II

  • Luzon
