Lineage

Constituted 13 June 1945 in the Army of the United States as the 596th Joint Assault Signal Company

Activated 20 June 1945 at Fort Jackson, South Carolina

Inactivated 25 September 1945 at Camp Callan, California

Redesignated 20 July 1951 as the 596th Signal Support Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 6 August 1951 at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Reorganized and redesignated 15 June 1954 as the 596th Signal Company

Inactivated 1 April 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 16 December 1995 at Fort Riley, Kansas, as an element of the 1st Armored Division

Inactivated 15 July 2006 at Fort Riley, Kansas, and relieved from assignment to the 1st Armored Division

Activated 16 October 2010 at Fort Belvoir, Virginia
