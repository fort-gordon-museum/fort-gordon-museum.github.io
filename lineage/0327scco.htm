Lineage and Honors

327th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 23 December 2010

Lineage

Constituted 17 June 1942 in the Army of the United States as the 327th Signal Company, Wing

Activated 15 July 1942 at Salt Lake City, Utah

Inactivated 28 October 1946 at Colorado Springs, Colorado

Redesignated 31 August 1966 as the 327th Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 25 November 1966 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Inactivated 31 March 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 1 July 1974 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Germany

Activated 16 October 2010 at Fort Detrick, Maryland
