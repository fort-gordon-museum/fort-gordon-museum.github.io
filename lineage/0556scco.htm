Lineage and Honors

556th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 14 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 3 February 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 556th Signal Depot Company

Activated 1 April 1944 at Camp Polk, Louisiana

Inactivated 31 October 1946 at Honolulu, Hawaii

Redesignated 27 September 1951 as the 556th Signal Radio Relay Company

Activated 10 November 1951 in Korea

Inactivated 12 March 1953 in Korea

Redesignated 15 November 1957 as the 556th Signal Company

Activated 15 March 1960 in Korea

Inactivated 15 December 1962 in Korea

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 26 January 1972 in Vietnam

Activated 16 April 1993 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Inactivated 15 September 1998 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Activated 16 October 2005 at Schofield Barracks, Hawaii and assigned to the 2d Brigade Combat Team, 25th Infantry Division
