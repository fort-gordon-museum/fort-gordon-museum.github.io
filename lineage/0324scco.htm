Lineage and Honors

324th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 23 December 2010

Lineage

Constituted 6 January 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 324th Signal Company, Wing

Activated 10 January 1943 at Camp Pinedale, California

Inactivated 14 November 1945 at Camp Pinedale, California

Redesignated 31 August 1966 as the 324th Signal Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 24 December 1966 at Fort Hood, Texas

Inactivated 15 November 1969 in Vietnam

Activated 30 June 1971 in Thailand

Inactivated 1 January 1972 in Thailand

Activated 1 June 1974 in Germany

Inactivated 15 January 2003 in Germany

Activated 16 December 2004 at Fort Hood, Texas
