Signal

232d Signal Company

232d Signal Company

Constituted 14 April 1942 in the Army of the United States as the 232d Signal Operation Company

Activated 15 June 1942 at Camp Crowder, Missouri

Inactivated 10 April 1946 in Japan

Redesignated 12 September 1952 as the 232d Signal Support Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 1 November 1952 at Camp Gordon, Georgia

Reorganized and redesignated 26 May 1954 as the 232d Signal Company

Inactivated 1 August 1966 in Vietnam

Activated 20 August 1968 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Inactivated 31 December 1968 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 20 December 1971 in Vietnam

Activated 1 July 1974 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Germany

Consolidated 15 November 1994 with the 516th Signal Company (constituted 16 October 1990 in the Regular Army as the 516th Signal Company and activated at Fort Huachuca, Arizona) and consolidated unit designated as the 516th Signal Company

Inactivated 15 January 1998 at Fort Huachuca, Arizona

Redesignated 1 June 2017 as the 232d Signal Company

Activated 16 September 2018 in Germany
