Lineage and Honors

57th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 1 February 2011

Lineage

Constituted 4 December 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 3110th Signal Service Battalion

Activated 27 December 1943 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Redesignated 18 June 1947 as the 77th Signal Service Battalion

Reorganized and redesignated 20 October 1947 as the 57th Signal Service Company

Inactivated 5 March 1949 in Germany

Redesignated 5 February 1953 as the 57th Signal Support Company and allotted to the Regular Army

Activated 12 March 1953 in Korea

Reorganized and redesignated 15 April 1954 as the 57th Signal Company

Inactivated 13 May 1955 in Korea

Activated 8 August 1957 in Korea

Inactivated 15 November 1967 in Korea

Activated 24 February 1969 in Vietnam

Inactivated 1 April 1970 in Vietnam

Activated 16 February 1973 at Fort Richardson, Alaska

Inactivated 15 April 1986 at Fort Richardson, Alaska

Activated 16 August 1992 in Germany

Inactivated 15 September 1994 in Germany

Activated 16 February 2006 in Germany, and assigned to the 2d Brigade Combat Team, 1st Infantry Division

Relieved 16 March 2008 from assignment to the 2d Brigade Combat Team, 1st Infantry Division, and assigned to the 172d Infantry Brigade
