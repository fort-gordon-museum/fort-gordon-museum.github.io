Lineage and Honors

579th SIGNAL COMPANY

Lineage and honors as of 20 December 2011

Lineage

Constituted 26 November 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 579th Signal Depot Company

Activated 20 December 1943 at Camp Cooke, California

Inactivated 25 March 1946 in Germany

Activated 1 June 1949 on Okinawa

Inactivated 1 July 1950 on Okinawa

Activated 21 September 1950 at Atlanta, Georgia

Allotted 16 July 1951 to the Regular Army

Reorganized and redesignated 10 November 1954 as the 579th Signal Company

Inactivated 15 December 1958 at Tobyhanna Signal Depot, Pennsylvania

Activated 1 March 1970 in Vietnam

Inactivated 30 April 1971 in Vietnam

Activated 16 November 2006 in Korea
