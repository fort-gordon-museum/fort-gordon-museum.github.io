Lineage

Constituted 26 November 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 54th Signal Repair Company

Activated 30 December 1943 at Camp Polk, Louisiana

Inactivated 5 April 1946 on Okinawa

Activated 22 March 1950 at San Antonio General Depot, Texas

Allotted 12 July 1951 to the Regular Army

Redesignated 2 November 1953 as the 54th Signal Company

Inactivated 20 July 1966 in Vietnam

Activated 1 December 1976 in Italy

Inactivated 15 September 1993 in Italy

Activated 16 June 2006 at Fort Hood, Texas
