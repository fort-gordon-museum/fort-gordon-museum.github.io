Lineage and Honors

14th Signal Detachment

Lineage and honors as of 12 March 2003

Lineage

Constituted 18 February 1944 in the Army of the United States as the 14th Signal Radar Maintenance Unit, Type A

Activated 25 April 1944 at Camp Davis, North Carolina

Inactivated 25 February 1946 in France

Redesignated 25 January 1949 as the 14th Signal Radar Maintenance Unit

Allotted to the Regular Army 15 February 1949

Activated 26 February 1949 at Fort Bliss, Texas

Reorganized and redesignated 12 March 1953 as the 14th Signal Detachment

Inactivated 25 June 1958 in Korea

Activated 15 December 1958 at Fort Lee, Virginia

Inactivated 25 April 1966 at Fort Lewis, Washington

Activated 25 November 1966 at Fort Carson, Colorado

Inactivated 23 June 1971 at Fort Carson, Colorado

Activated 16 October 2002 in Korea
