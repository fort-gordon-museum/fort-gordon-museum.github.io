Lineage and Honors

804th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 30 July 2003

Lineage

Constituted 26 March 1943 in the Army of the United States as the 295th Signal Company

Activated 3 April 1943 at Camp Bradford, Virginia

Reorganized and redesignated 11 November 1943 as the 295th Joint Assault Signal Company

Inactivated 10 October 1945 in the Philippine Islands

Redesignated 6 May 1947 as the 804th Joint Assault Signal Company and allotted to the Organized Reserves

Activated 2 June 1947 at Pasadena, California

(Organized Reserves redesignated 25 March 1948 as the Organized Reserve Corps; redesignated 9 July 1952 as the Army Reserve)

Inactivated 1 November 1948 at Pasadena, California

Redesignated 6 June 1950 as the 804th Signal Radio Company

Activated 2 July 1950 at Jersey City, New Jersey

Inactivated 6 May 1952 at Jersey City, New Jersey

Redesignated 16 March 1954 as the 804th Signal Company

Activated 1 May 1954 at New York, New York

Inactivated 1 February 1956 at New York, New York

Activated 16 April 1985 at Fort DeRussy, Hawaii

Location changed 1 September 1994 to Fort Shafter, Hawaii
