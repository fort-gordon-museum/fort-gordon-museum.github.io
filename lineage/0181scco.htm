Lineage

Constituted 8 January 1946 in the Army of the United States as the 181st Signal Depot Company

Activated 21 January 1946 at Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Allotted 25 October 1951 to the Regular Army

Reorganized and redesignated 15 April 1954 as the 181st Signal Company

Inactivated 25 April 1960 in Korea

Activated 25 March 1961 in Korea

Inactivated 15 November 1967 in Korea

Activated 1 June 1974 in Germany
