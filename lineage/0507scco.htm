Lineage and Honors

507th Signal Company

Lineage and honors as of 22 July 2004

Lineage

Constituted 9 April 1945 in the Army of the United States as the 4030th Signal Service Company

Activated 27 April 1945 at Fort Monmouth, New Jersey

Redesignated 20 March 1947 as the 507th Signal Service Company

Inactivated 2 June 1949 at Vint Hill Farms Station, Virginia

Activated 25 January 1950 in Japan

Inactivated 15 March 1950 in Japan

Activated 5 September 1950 in Japan

Allotted 25 October 1951 to the Regular Army

Reorganized and redesignated 20 March 1953 as the 507th Signal Company

Inactivated 24 June 1955 in Korea

Activated 15 September 1957 in France

Inactivated 19 August 1963 in France

Activated 16 October 1992 at Fort Greely, Alaska
